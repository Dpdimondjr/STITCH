Sectors per star
STITCH models p(flux_offset | context) with an 18D context vector (detector position, star properties, sector) using a Neural Spline Flow. Applied as a per-sector multiplicative scale factor on PDCSAP flux. Confidence-weighted inference falls back to no-op for uncertain predictions.

Training Label Extraction

How each (star, sector) pair becomes a training data point. No external labels — the signal comes entirely from each star's own multi-sector history. Illustrated on a simulated 5-sector star; sector 18 is the evaluation target.

The LOO label is self-supervised: the star itself is the reference. Label quality improves with more sectors — with 4 sectors the reference mean is noisy; with 20+ it is extremely stable. Stars with fewer than 4 sectors are excluded entirely.

Pipeline

End-to-end flow from MAST to trained model.

TARS catalogRandom-forest sys_score per (TIC, sector). Filter sys_score > 0.95, Tmag ≤ 13, ≥ 2 quiet sectors. Yields 37K training stars. MAST / lightkurveDownload TESS-SPOC HLSP FITS. Author=TESS-SPOC, exptime≥100s. collect_topup.pyPer (star, sector): compute median(PDCSAP_FLUX) after quality masking. Extract header features. Delete FITS. LOO labelflux_offset(s,i) = median(s,i) / mean{ median(s,j) : j≠i }. Filter 0.85–1.15, min 4 sectors. train_flow_nsf.py80/10/10 star-level split by camera. Weights=clip(n_sectors,20)/20. NLL minimisation. zuko.flows.NSF8 spline transforms, 16 bins, hidden=[256,256], 666K params. Adam 3e-4, patience=25. Inference300 posterior samples → mean±std → weight=1/(1+5×std) → blended correction.
